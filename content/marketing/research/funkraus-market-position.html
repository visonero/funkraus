Where funkraus stands in the German BZF market

A review of the online BZF I/II preparation market: who else is selling to the same student, how funkraus compares on offer, SEO and content, design and trust — and what it takes to go from "a good course" to "a market threat."

B–
Strong product, close to zero visibility
Product and content are already competitive. Search presence is not — that's the entire gap.

On paper, funkraus already has one of the most complete offers in this niche: a single course covering both BZF I and BZF II, a free 2-module trial with no card required, the full 261-question official catalogue, a live AI radio-training feature, a real 100-question/60-minute mock exam for both tracks, and 15 published articles — more content than every competitor except one. No other provider we found combines a fully structured self-paced course with AI voice training and a proper mock exam under one price.

The catch: a site:funkraus.de search returns zero indexed pages. None of that product or content strength shows up in Google yet. funkraus isn't losing to competitors on quality — it's simply not in the race yet on the channel that matters most (organic search), while at least one competitor (bzf-kurs.de) already has real content-SEO traction and reviews, and the "AI radio training" pitch itself is no longer unique — three to four other tools now market a version of it.

1The competitive landscape

Two genuinely different groups compete for the same searches. Tier A sells a full BZF I/II preparation the way funkraus does. Tier B sells one narrow piece of it (just the question catalogue, or just AI radio practice) — they don't replace a course, but they intercept the same searches and can expand later.

4Current standing: what's working, what isn't

🟢 Real strengths

  • Broadest single offer in the niche. No other provider bundles BZF I + II, a free trial, AI voice training, and a real 100Q/60min mock exam under one price.
  • Content volume already leads Tier A. 15 published articles beats every full-course competitor except bzf-kurs.de's combined post+pillar count.
  • Transparent, low-friction pricing. €279 one-time, visible upfront, free 2 modules with no card — most competitors hide price behind a contact form or checkout flow.
  • Modern design and execution. Custom illustrations, animation, and a coherent brand — several direct competitors (Fluglehrerteam, Training4Pilots' course pages) look dated by comparison.

🔴 Real gaps

  • Not indexed yet. site:funkraus.de returns zero results. Every content and product advantage above is invisible to search until this is fixed.
  • Zero trust signals. No reviews, testimonials, or case studies, versus bzf-kurs.de's 44 Trustpilot reviews and Fluglehrerteam's "since 2005" longevity.
  • The AI pitch alone is no longer unique. Three to four competitors (FunkCoach, bzf-simulator.de, bzftraining.de) already market AI radio training — the differentiator has to be the complete package, not the feature in isolation.
  • No backlinks yet. None of the research surfaced funkraus.de being linked from anywhere — no forum mentions, no directory listings, no partner links (the new /partner page is the first lever for this).

5How many blog articles is "enough"?

Short answer: volume was never the bottleneck — indexation is. At 15 articles, funkraus already has more content than every full-course competitor except bzf-kurs.de (7 posts + ~5 pillar pages, ~12 pieces total). BZF is a narrow niche with limited search volume; nobody in this market has more than about a dozen content pieces. Publishing 100 articles wouldn't out-rank anyone if the pages behind them aren't crawled.

That said, there's a concrete ceiling worth reaching to own the topic outright — not by writing more, but by writing a few pieces no competitor has:

Now → immediately Fix indexation first

Confirm all 15 URLs are submitted in Search Console, check for crawl errors, and get the first backlinks flowing (the /partner page's mutual links are the fastest lever). No amount of new content matters until this resolves.

15 → ~22 articles Close the comparison gap

Add the handful of pieces bzf-kurs.de has that funkraus doesn't: a direct "online vs. Präsenzlehrgang" comparison, a cost-comparison page naming providers, and a BZF glossary page targeting single-term searches ("QNH", "Blindsendung", "PAN PAN MEDICAL") that a full article wouldn't rank for on its own.

~22 → ~30 pieces Own the long tail

Round out remaining exam-catalogue topics (transponder codes 7500, radio-failure edge cases already covered, weather phraseology/ATIS/VOLMET, BZF for glider/UL pilots) and add proof pages once available: student testimonials, a partner-club case study, a "why we built the AI tower" behind-the-scenes piece.

30 well-targeted pieces would give funkraus roughly 2.5× the content depth of the best-optimized competitor (bzf-kurs.de) — more than sufficient to dominate this specific niche, since no one else is even close to that volume.
